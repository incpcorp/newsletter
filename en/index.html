About DI Forum

DI Forum (Data Integrity Forum) is a public channel on data integrity for the pharmaceutical and biotech industry in Korea. It brings together a newsletter, short regulatory updates, records of the annual Data Integrity Forum and public presentation materials in one place.

Our topics include data integrity and ALCOA++, computerized system compliance, data governance, and how regulatory expectations from the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS), FDA, EMA and PIC/S apply on the shop floor. We also follow Pharma 4.0, ESG and AI governance as they relate to trustworthy data.

DI Forum is planned and operated by I&CP Co., Ltd. (opens in a new tab), a consulting firm specializing in data integrity and computerized system compliance.

Data Integrity Forum

An annual forum on data integrity held at COEX in Seoul. It combines commentary on regulatory trends with practical case studies from Korean companies.

2026
3rd Forum · 22 Sep 2026Data Integrity by DesignDesign and expansion: DI by Design, ESG and AI governance

Three modules (Foundation, Solution, Expansion) asked how far data integrity now reaches: from laboratory chromatograms to equipment sensor values, validation records and the data used to train AI models. Sessions covered the regulatory direction behind DI by Design, trustworthy data as the basis of ESG, digital validation tools, and AI use and verification in regulated pharmaceutical work.

2025
2nd Forum · 1 Oct 2025EU GMP UpdateRegulatory deep dive and digital GMP

An in-depth look at the EU GMP revisions to Chapter 4 and Annex 11 and the new Annex 22 on AI. Discussion points: the regulatory focus moving from managing data to how data is created; audit trails extending to alarm logs; cloud and SaaS responsibilities and data flow diagrams; electronic signatures and multi-factor authentication; and Annex 22 allowing only static, deterministic models for critical GMP use, under human oversight.

The first forum laid the groundwork: GMP history, 21 CFR Part 11 and ALCOA as the basic framework; IT/OT infrastructure and application-level CSV strategies presented from the supplier side; and experience from dedicated DI teams at two Korean pharmaceutical companies, from manufacturing and quality perspectives.
